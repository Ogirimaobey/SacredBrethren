GATHERING MEN.
IGNITING FIRE.

What started in 2022 as a simple gathering of four friends in Obangede, Kogi State, united by a burning desire to call upon the name of the Lord, has grown into a spiritual movement.

Sacred Brethren is not just a meeting—it is a cry for revival. Over the past five years, God has expanded our borders across Kogi State, Abuja, and Lagos, giving us the privilege of hosting over 145+ programs spanning virtual bible studies, physical meetings, school outreaches, community supports, and soul-harvesting evangelism touching over 2,000+ lives.
